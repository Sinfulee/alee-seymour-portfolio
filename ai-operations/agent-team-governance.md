# Running a ten-agent AI team with approval gates (outline)

- **Problem:** many parallel projects needed specialist agents that would not overstep or act on guesses.
- **Approach:** a coordinator plus nine specialists, each with a written mission and write scope; a gates table of who may decide what; a decisions register with sources.
- **Iterations:**
  - Approval relayed through the coordinator was refused, so spending approval moved to the spending agent's own channel.
  - Stale status reports led to a rule: check the live state before claiming anything is done.
- **Evaluation:** register rows cite sources; items are live-checked before they are marked closed.
- **Results:** daily public activity ran with a human yes on each post; a register of closed and denied items stops repeat proposals.
- **Limitations:** usage limits paused work more than once; one scheduled routine used 21% of a week's usage, which led to trimming it.
