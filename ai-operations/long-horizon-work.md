# Long-horizon iterative work (outline)

Across 577 archived AI conversations from 2023 to 2026, I return to the same problems over weeks and months. The loop repeats: research, give it a structure, reach a working conclusion, come back later and refine it. Counts come from conversation titles and dates; some conversations fall under two topics.

| Topic | Conversations | Span | Typical return / longest gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prompt, bot and image-prompt building | 21 | May 2023 to Sep 2026 | 29 days / 296 days |
| Worldbuilding and game systems | 27 | Aug 2023 to Jul 2026 | 22 days / 154 days |
| Fiction and long-form writing | 19 | Mar 2023 to Aug 2026 | 75 days / 194 days |
| Science research documents | 13 | Jun to Jul 2026 | 4 days / 10 days |
| Local AI and a personal knowledge vault | 12 | May 2024 to Oct 2026 | 7 days / 735 days |
| Knowledge pages and traffic | 5 | Jun to Sep 2026 | 21 days / 35 days |
| Community network operations | 33 | Jan 2024 to Aug 2026 | 12 days / 352 days |

- **How the work moved (examples):**
  - Prompt building: templates and structure, then defined terms and versioned drafts, then critique rounds.
  - Research documents: scope per tab, an overclaim check, then a self-review at the end of each session.
  - Local AI: a server idea, a two-year gap, a failed first bot, then a working vault.
