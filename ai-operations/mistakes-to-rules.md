# Mistakes log: every incident becomes a rule (outline)

- **Problem:** agent mistakes repeat unless the cause becomes a written rule.
- **Approach:** each incident is logged with what happened, the root cause, the cost and the rule going forward.
- **Examples:**
  - A page redirect cost about twenty times its quote. Rule: confirm the target title exists before acting.
  - A reply was posted twice after it failed to show on screen. Rule: never retry before checking the permalink, the replies and the activity feed.
  - An approval note applied twice cut a sentence. Rule: hand over the verbatim final text.
  - A post went live with no working link. Rule: a five-point posting standard (link in two places, images, private test, read-back).
- **Evaluation:** whether the same failure came back after the rule.
- **Results:** 10 numbered lessons and a dated log, with rules cross-referenced.
- **Limitations:** recurrence has not been measured formally.
