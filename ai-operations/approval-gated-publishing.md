# Approval-gated publishing (outline)

- **Problem:** drafting posts and replies at volume without losing the author's voice or publishing anything unapproved.
- **Where:** Substack articles and notes, Tangled posts.
- **Approach:** written voice rules and reply rules, a comparison of each human edit against the draft, a posting standard, and one live read-back after each post.
- **Iterations:**
  - A promotional post went live with no working link and was deleted, which produced the five-point posting standard.
  - A comment landed on the wrong post, so every public write now states author, title, ID and group before it is sent.
- **Evaluation:** a live read-back after each post; comparisons of human edits against drafts.
- **Results:** five drafting lessons drawn from human edits; ten verified comments in one day (Nov 9, 2026); no post goes out without an explicit yes.
- **Limitations:** no A/B tests; engagement is small and mixed with other factors.
